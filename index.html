About CampusCash

CampusCash is a small money manager made for students. Add what comes in from a job or home, log what you spend, and see what’s left, month by month.

Everything is saved only in this browser, on this device. There are no accounts, no ads, and nothing is uploaded.

Budgets are optional. Use one if a limit helps you, and ignore it if it doesn’t.
